FAQ

Questions bands ask.

Is Guatemala safe?

Antigua is one of Central America's most visited cities. You'll have private transport for the whole trip, a house of your own, and guides we know personally for every excursion. We live here and will steer you toward the right places.

Where do we fly into?

La Aurora International Airport (GUA) in Guatemala City. There are direct flights from Houston, Miami, Atlanta, Dallas, LA, New York and more. We pick you up there, and the drive to the house is about an hour.

Do we need a visa?

Visitors from the US, Canada, the UK and the EU generally don't need a visa for short stays. Check the requirements for your own passport before booking.

What should we bring?

Your guitars, basses, pedals and anything that's core to your sound. We provide mics, preamps, monitoring, headphones, amps and keys. We'll send a full gear list during planning.

What about drums?

We can program drums, and live drum options can be arranged. Tell us about your project and we'll find the best fit.

Can we do more than 6 songs?

Yes. Add extra nights or songs, or ask about a full-album retreat.

Is mastering included?

Yes. Every mix is mastered to a level ready for streaming.

What files do we get?

For each song you get the stems, the full mix and the master.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Everything is run in English.
